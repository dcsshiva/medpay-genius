# Architecture decisions
- Keep Overview unit-attendance aggregation in a pure module with shared daily denominators; stacked unit contributions must preserve the overall attendance percentage.
- Keep retired doctor, visit, and non-payroll payment screens behind one shared client-side screen filter in navigation, permissions UI, and tab dispatch; this preserves historical data and backend privileges while making the remix staff-focused.
- The app UI is the approved Selvantra HRMS prototype, ported verbatim: `scripts/prototype/index.html` → `scripts/port_prototype.py` (asserted text patches) → `src/hrms/prototype.ts`. Change screens/logic by editing the patch script (or the prototype) and regenerating — never hand-edit `prototype.ts`.
- `src/hrms/HrmsApp.tsx` hosts it (routes `/`, `/dashboard`, `/auth`); data lives in the `hr_*` tables (migration 0013, demo seed 0014) and is saved by diffing the in-memory collections (`src/hrms/data.ts`). Staff sign in as `<code>@staff.westmed.local` via the `hr-manage-login` edge function; admins use their WestMed accounts. Old screens stay at `/classic`.
- Payslip figures (earnings split, EPF on Basic, ESI, employer contribution, one-cycle adjustments) live in the pure `src/hrms/payslip.js` module with node tests; per-staff structure and per-cycle adjustments are jsonb on `hr_staff` so finalized `hr_payslips.summary` freezes them.
- Shift plan files: layout, checks and change list live in the pure `src/hrms/shiftPlan.js` (node tests); `src/hrms/shiftPlanFile.js` writes/reads .xlsx/.xls/.csv (lazy-loaded). Managers write `hr_roster` only for their reports, today onward, unfinalized cycles (RLS, migration 0021); a roster `WO` is a planned weekly off read by `normalizePunches(…, {plan})`.
- Withdrawing approved leave/permission: window and attendance restore rules live in the pure `src/hrms/leaveRevoke.js` (node tests); approval saves the day on `hr_leaves.prev_attendance`; the `hr_leaves_guard` trigger (migration 0022) enforces Approved → Revoked only, running/previous cycle only, not in a finalized cycle.
- Multi-unit staff: gap and coverage rules live in the pure `src/hrms/multiUnit.js` (node tests); `hr_staff.multi_unit` and `hr_import_log` (migration 0023). The import merge itself stays in `mergeAttendanceRecord` (punches.js).
- Weekly off with effective dates: `weeklyOffOn(base, history, date)` in `src/hrms/punches.js`; `hr_staff.weekly_off` is the day from the start, `hr_weekly_off` rows change it from `effective_from` (managers: own staff, today onward — RLS, migration 0024). Every `normalizePunches` call passes `weeklyOffHistory`. It also passes `shiftContext(staff, SHIFT_MASTER)`: a lone evening punch starts a night shift only when a night shift is planned that day (or nothing is planned and the person is not on a General Shift); otherwise it is the OUT of a day with a missing IN.
- Top administrator as reporting manager: `hr_staff.reporting_manager = '@top'` (TOP_MGR, port_prototype.py section 55) means the Payroll Settings "Top administrator" (no staff code); offered in every reporting-manager list, resolved by `topManagerFor`, and their requests go to administrators.
- Approved leave set by the reporting manager: leave type and attendance status `AL` (migration 0027, trigger `hr_leaves_al_guard`: running/previous cycle, up to today, not finalized); `absencesForLeave` and the window live in the pure `src/hrms/leaveRevoke.js` (node tests). An `AL` day is paid and does not use CL; it is withdrawn like any approval. The same dialog sets a week off: leave type `WO` (migration 0028) whose effect is a past-day roster `WO` (managers may insert/remove only `WO` on past days in that window — RLS); withdrawing removes that roster `WO`.
- Admin notifications: `hr_notifications` (migration 0026) holds tasks for one administrator account (`recipient_email`; RLS: an admin reads and updates only their own, any admin can send), each with steps, staff items and the admin tab to open. The bell in the admin header (port_prototype.py section 54) lists them and marks them read or done.
- Help: the English/Tamil text for every screen is pure data in `src/hrms/helpContent.js` (`HELP`, `HELP_ORDER`, `helpKey`; node tests check every entry has both languages). The Help · உதவி button (top bar and sign-in card, port_prototype.py section 58) shows the entry for the screen in view, About, or all screens, and prints the whole document. Add or update an entry whenever a screen changes. The Selvantra logo lives in `public/selvantra/`.
- App updates on phones / installed copies: `HrmsApp.tsx` compares its build version with `/build-info.json` (never cached) at start, when the app returns to the screen and every 10 minutes; the decision lives in the pure `src/hrms/appUpdate.js` (node tests). A newer version reloads straight away when nothing would be lost (signed out, or no unsaved changes / dialog / typing), clearing the service worker and caches; otherwise an "Update now" bar shows. A version already tried in the tab only shows the bar (no reload loop). Staff see "Version … · Released …" (`versionLine`, section 62) in the phone account menu, under the desktop staff header and in Help, with "Check for update" (window event `hr-check-update` → HrmsApp updates now or says it is the latest).
- Shared lists and settings are readable only by organisation members (`is_org_member`, never `USING (true)`); `hr_payroll_runs` totals are admin/manager-only and staff get finalized status from `hr_payroll_run_status` — keeps company payroll figures and staff data away from unrelated signed-in accounts.
- Installing on phones: the manifest (vite.config.ts) installs the app as "Selvantra HRMS" with the Selvantra icons in `public/selvantra/` (`icon-192/512`, `icon-maskable-512`, `apple-touch-icon`), `start_url` `/?source=pwa`. The install card (sign-in page + staff dashboard, phones only, 7-day "Not now"), the bilingual iPhone steps and the admin "App link · QR" (lazy `qrcode-generator`) are port_prototype.py section 59. The link HR sends is `/install` (`src/hrms/InstallPage.tsx`): per phone it shows one-tap install (Android), Safari Add to Home Screen steps (iPhone), "open in Safari" (other iPhone browsers) or a QR (computers); `main.tsx` keeps an early `beforeinstallprompt` in `window.__installPrompt`. Dialogs on the sign-in page need `.hr-auth .modal-back` above `.auth`.
- Staff portal on phones (≤720px): root class `staff-phone` (and `staff-only` for non-managers) from port_prototype.py section 60 — compact header with an account menu, the module menu as a bottom tab bar, a Today card + next 7 days (pure `src/hrms/staffToday.js`, node tests: holiday > approved leave > Shift Planner > Staff Master), and day / task / leave cards instead of tables. Desktop and the admin view are unchanged.
- Tamil for the employee screens: the pure `src/hrms/staffTamil.js` (`TA` phrases + patterns, node tests) is applied by a MutationObserver to whatever the staff view, its menu and its dialogs render (port_prototype.py section 61); the தமிழ் / English button (top bar on staff screens, sign-in card) is stored per device (`hrStaffLang`). Admin screens stay English. New staff-facing text should get a `TA` entry.
- Leave updates for employees: the pure `src/hrms/staffNotices.js` (node tests) lists decisions from the last 14 days not yet seen on this device (`hrLeaveSeen:<empNo>`); shown on Home and as a count on the Leave tab, cleared by opening Leave or dismissing.
- Fewer server requests (pure rules in `src/hrms/listCache.js`, node tests): shared lists (units, departments, designations, shifts, holidays, task templates, settings, staff directory — never full staff rows) are kept per device/account/app version in localStorage and re-downloaded only when `hr_data_version_stamps()` (migration 0029, statement triggers on those tables) says they changed; leave loads from the previous pay cycle onward + pending (`ensureLeavesFrom` adds older cycles when opened); admins read attendance/roster in 3 parallel pages; Overview trends use `hr_attendance_stats_many`; coming back after 15 min runs `store.refreshLive` (day-to-day data only) unless something is being edited. A new shared table that the app caches needs the trigger in 0029.
- Shift plan file and reporting managers: the Reporting Manager column is editable in administrators' .xlsx exports and read on upload (`row.manager`); `managerResolver` (shiftPlan.js, tests) turns a name, Staff Code, "NAME (code)" or the top administrator's name into a code; admin uploads list `managerChanges` in the preview and save them to Staff Master (blank keeps the current manager); managers' uploads never change it.
- Hours worked: pure `src/hrms/workHours.js` (tests); `summary.workedMin` in computeCycle, shown as "Hours worked" on staff pages, an Hours column, day cards and the Shift Ledger (section 65). Attendance and roster load one day either side of the pay cycle so a night on the last day pairs with the next morning's OUT; pay-cycle figures still count only the cycle's days.
- Department-wise shifts: `hr_shifts.allowed_departments` (migration 0032; department codes and `@managers` = role Manager) and the pure `src/hrms/shiftScope.js` (`shiftAllowed`, tests) behind `shiftAllowedFor`: a department named on any shift gets only its shifts plus the open ones; a department named on none gets all. The planner legend and .xlsx drop-down use `plannerShifts()` (shifts usable by the staff in view); plan uploads check allowed shifts only on changed cells. Shift Master edits it via the Departments column (section 66).
- Late arrivals and double shifts: pure `src/hrms/lateRule.js` (tests). In computeCycle a late day = late beyond grace and the extra-late allowance, not fully covered by approved permission; every `lateLopEvery`-th (3) costs `lateLopDays` (½) × perDay (Payroll Settings) — no per-minute deduction. `hr_shifts.pay_days` (migration 0033) > 1 is a double shift: counts as extra days (`summary.extraDays`, `extraDayPay` in net and the payslip's EXTRA SHIFT line), only where planned — `effectiveShift`/`nearestShift` never guess it. Overnight shifts (end ≤ start) planned for a date: `shiftContext().overnight` lets a lone punch from 3 h before start open the night and the next day's punch close it up to end + 2 h (punches.js). Section 67.
- Phone start-up (no blank screens): `index.html` shows a loading screen at once and runs a plain-ES5 safety net before the app — in-memory storage when localStorage/sessionStorage are blocked (`window.__storageBlocked` → note from main.tsx), a too-old-browser check, and a bilingual card with Reload when the app script fails to download or throws before `window.__hrStarted` (set by main.tsx); slow-network note after 12 s. The stylesheet loads non-blocking (`nonBlockingCssPlugin` in vite.config.ts, link#hr-css; main.tsx waits for it). Keep the first download small: /classic and public pages are `lazy()` in App.tsx and jsPDF loads inside `payslipPDF` (section 68); a missing lazy chunk after an update reloads once (`vite:preloadError`).
- Files in the admin bell: `hr_notifications.files` ([{name,type,size,url?}]) and `file_data` (base64, same order; migration 0035) — listed on the card with Download (section 69). Contents come from `env.notificationFile` (fetched only on download, recipient-only by RLS) or, with `url`, from a same-site file shipped in `public/` (e.g. `public/docs/` checklists — no staff or pay data there, it is publicly reachable).
